Meet the founder

Hi, I'm Dr Irina.

I'm a neuroscientist with a background in psychology. I founded NeuroFusion Daily because I believe that science can help us understand ourselves without making us feel like projects that constantly need improving.

Here, we explore the brain, behaviour, sleep and emotional life through accessible writing and thoughtful practices. The goal is not perfect optimisation. It's feeling a little more grounded in the life you already have.
